Admission

Entry requirements

Applicants must satisfy one of the following pathways AND hold a credit in Additional Mathematics at SPM level (or equivalent).

i
Pass in Matriculation or Foundation in Computing / Science, Technology and Engineering with a minimum CGPA of 2.00.
ii
Pass in STPM with a minimum Grade C (GP 2.00) in Mathematics and one Science / ICT subject.
iii
Diploma (MQF Level 4) in Artificial Intelligence / Computing with minimum CGPA 2.50. Candidates with CGPA 2.00–2.49 may be admitted subject to rigorous assessment.
iv
Diploma in a related Science, Technology or Engineering field (MQF Level 4) with minimum CGPA 2.75. Candidates with CGPA 2.50–2.74 may be admitted subject to rigorous assessment.
v
Diploma Kemahiran Malaysia (DKM) / Diploma Vokasional Malaysia (DVM) in related fields with CGPA 2.50–2.74, subject to rigorous assessment.
vi
Diploma Lanjutan Kemahiran Malaysia (DLKM) in related fields with CGPA 2.50–2.74, subject to rigorous assessment.
vii
Other relevant qualifications recognised by the Malaysian Government.
